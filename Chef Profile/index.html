About the Chef

Global Private Chef and Restaurant Consultant

Christian Biscocho is a globally experienced private chef and restaurant consultant with over 25 years in luxury hospitality, fine dining, and Michelin-guide environments. A graduate of San Diego Culinary Arts Institute, he specializes in delivering bespoke culinary experiences and high-level restaurant strategy for discerning clients seeking world-class standards.

Private Chef for Exclusive Dining

Christian provides discreet, fully customized private chef services for high-net-worth individuals, luxury residences, and VIP events worldwide.

  • Personal chef for private dinners and estate dining
  • Luxury tasting menus tailored to dietary goals
  • Travel chef services internationally
  • Wellness, performance, and lifestyle cuisine
  • High-end event dining

Restaurant Consultant for Luxury and Fine Dining Concepts

As a restaurant consultant, Christian helps hospitality brands launch, refine, and elevate their culinary operations for profitability, prestige, and guest loyalty.

  • Restaurant concept development
  • Menu engineering and costing strategy
  • Kitchen systems and workflow optimization
  • Staff training and luxury service standards
  • Pre-opening and relaunch consulting

His leadership tenure with Wolfgang Puck Worldwide included contributions to acclaimed venues such as Spago Beverly Hills and CUT Beverly Hills, as well as culinary involvement at Hotel Bel-Air.
