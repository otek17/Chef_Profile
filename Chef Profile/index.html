About the Chef

Private Chef Services & Hospitality Consulting

I'm Chef Christian, and for over 25 years I've been obsessed with creating meals and experiences that people never forget.

My path has led me through some of the most respected kitchens in luxury hospitality—from Wolfgang Puck's restaurant group and Spago Beverly Hills to Hotel Bel-Air, Shutters on the Beach, Soho House West Hollywood, and Loews Coronado Bay. I've cooked on private yachts, in exclusive residences, and everywhere in between.

Today I bring that same level of care and precision directly to you—whether it's an intimate dinner at home, a family celebration, a corporate retreat, or a deeper hospitality consulting project.

Private Chef Services

My goal is simple: make your life easier while delivering food that feels personal, effortless, and genuinely delicious.

I work closely with you to design menus that match your tastes, dietary needs, and vision—whether that's a beautiful family dinner, weekly meal prep, a special-occasion spread, or a multi-course tasting experience. Every event is different, and your dining experience should be too.

Restaurant & Hospitality Consulting

For restaurant owners, hospitality pros, and entrepreneurs, I help turn strong ideas into successful, profitable realities.

With decades of hands-on experience in luxury operations, I provide practical support on menu development, kitchen systems, staff training, guest experience, concept refinement, and everything it takes to run a smooth, standout operation.

Why Clients Choose Me

Great food matters, but what I care about most is creating space for you to relax, connect with the people around your table, and actually enjoy the moment.

Luxury doesn't have to feel cold or intimidating. The best hospitality feels warm, thoughtful, and human. That's the standard I bring to every dinner I cook and every project I consult on.

I'd be honored to be part of your next gathering or help elevate your hospitality vision. Let's create something memorable together.
